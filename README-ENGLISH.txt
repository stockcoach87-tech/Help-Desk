HOW TO PUBLISH THE WEBSITE
1. Extract the website ZIP file on your computer.
2. Upload all files from this folder to your hosting provider's public web root. This folder is often named public_html, www, or wwwroot.
3. Open your domain using HTTPS and test the navigation links, phone number, email address, and contact form.
4. The contact form opens the visitor's email application. It does not send or store form submissions by itself. If you want a hosted form, connect a reputable form-processing service and update the Privacy Policy to describe how submitted information is handled.
5. Before publishing, confirm that the business name, actual services, phone number, email address, and any listed address accurately reflect your business and official records.
6. The Udyam certificate you provided lists the enterprise name as HELP DESK and the NIC activity as 56302 (Tea/Coffee Shops). If your actual business provides digital support, resolve any inaccurate or outdated registration details through the official Udyam process. Do not present inconsistent information to platforms.
7. Remove or replace any contact details that should not be public. Once your final domain and business details are confirmed, add a domain-specific sitemap.xml, a business email address, and any legally required disclosures as appropriate.
